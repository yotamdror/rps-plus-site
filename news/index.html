· November 24

A note to Cohort Zero

Thank you for being part of Cohort Zero.

RPS+ is meant to be extremely easy to learn, not very hard to beat, and very difficult to get a high score in. My hope is that the loop itself is fun and addictive enough to keep you coming back, with enough strategy and luck to keep it engaging.

What I want to learn from you: do the Events make the game feel more strategic and add to the world, or are they disruptive? And is the loop fun, clear and easy? All feedback welcome.

25 cents a week for a screenshot that beats your old score, up to $2. Text it to me with your mailing address.

Release notes go up here every week. Once I figure out notifications or automated emails, you'll get weekly annoyances from those too.
